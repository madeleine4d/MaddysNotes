**Assumptions:**
- no selection
- no mutations
- population is large
Thus allele frequency is stable.

if there are two alleles for a given gene then $P+q = 1$
then $(P+q)^{2}=1^{2}$ represents the distribution of theses probabilities including heterozygotes.
thus…
$$P^{2}+2Pq + q^{2}=1$$
$P^2$ is the frequency of homozygous P (PP)
$2Pq$ is the frequency of the heterozygous (Pq)
$q^2$ is the frequency of homozygous q (qq)
