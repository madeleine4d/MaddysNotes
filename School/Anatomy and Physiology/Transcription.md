The process of “writing” DNA into (generally) mRNA. 
## in Eukaryotes
- DNA is transcribed into pre-mRNA before it is processed into mature mRNA
- RNA pol starts at a promoter
- separates the strands and advances from 5’ to 3’ 
- transcribes from the template strand to complementary RNA 
- stops at a terminator 
### processing
- add 5’ cap (modified guanine)
- add poly-a tail
- introns are removed
