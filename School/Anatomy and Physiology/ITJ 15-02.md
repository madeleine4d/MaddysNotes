## prayer 
2 schools of thought
- kevah – the nuts and bolts side of it
	- started with the order of the amidah
- kavanah - feeling, intentions, and meditations.
	- started with the psalms


obligations were kept after the destruction of the temple and some sacrifices were replaced with prayers

Ma’ariv evening prayer was added later and does not replace a sacrifice
